01 The team
Est. 2025

A student-led engineering team

We are the MechaTigers — FIRST Robotics Competition Team 10121 from Triangle Math and Science Academy in Apex, North Carolina. Each season we design, machine, wire, and program a competition robot in six weeks, then field it against teams from across the state.

Our mission is to inspire students to pursue STEM through hands-on engineering, gracious professionalism, and real-world problem solving. Students run every subteam — build, electrical, programming, marketing, and outreach — with mentors guiding rather than driving.

In our rookie year (2025, REEFSCAPE) we competed at two district events. One season later we were event finalists, then event winners, and qualified for the FIRST North Carolina State Championship.

04 Process
Kickoff → Field

How a robot gets built

From game reveal to the competition field, every discipline is student-run.

Build

Design & build

The build subteam takes the season's game and turns it into a machine — prototyping mechanisms, framing the chassis, and assembling every iteration in the TMSA workshop.

Fabrication

Fabrication

Cutting, drilling, and finishing the parts the design calls for — students do the shop work themselves, from lumber field elements to aluminum structure.

Electrical

Electrical

The electrical subteam wires power distribution, motor controllers, and sensors — the nervous system that has to survive full-contact matches.

Programming

Programming

Drive control, autonomous routines, and vision targeting — the programming subteam writes the code that turns hardware into a competitor.

Strategy

Strategy & scouting

Between matches the team scouts other robots, plans alliances, and adapts — competition is played as much on the whiteboard as on the field.

05 Community
Apex, NC

Outreach

Robots are the vehicle; the mission is getting more students — and their families — into engineering.

Fig. 05.1Robot exhibitions

We bring the competition robot to school and community events, letting younger students see it run — and drive it — up close.

Fig. 05.2Fall Festival

Running booths at the TMSA Apex Fall Festival — meeting parents and younger students where they already are.

Fig. 05.3Club Expo

Recruiting the next generation of members at TMSA's club expo — no experience required, every subteam open.

Fig. 05.4STEM nights & lessons

We host educational robotics lessons and STEM nights that introduce robotics — and FIRST competition — to parents and younger students.
